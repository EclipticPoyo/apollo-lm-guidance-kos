## 3.2 External delta-V (P30)

For your own burn: an ignition time (N33) and a delta-v (N81), checked with P30, then flown by P40, P42 or P41. The DOI checklist has these steps written out. P32 to P35 set their burns themselves: no P30.

```checklist
band: LOAD THE BURN | CARD: P30 LM Maneuver
- | DSKY | V25 N33 E, V25 N81 E (or the uplink)
n: The planner's SEND loads the DOI
band: CHECK THE BURN | P30
use: p30
- NEXT | FLY IT | Engine {@3-3} or RCS {@3-4}
```
