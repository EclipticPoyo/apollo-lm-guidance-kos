## 3.3 Engine burns (P40, P42)

For a 099 TPI flown on an engine ({@3-14}; the 210 TPI page, {@3-15}, has its steps written out), and for your own burns. The burn is already set: by P30 ({@3-2}), or by P32 to P35. The DOI checklist has the P40 steps written out.

```checklist
band: DESCENT ENGINE | P40  /  in lunar orbit
use: p40-ignition
- TIG+0:15 | TERMINAL | Raise throttle
- | SHIFT | Hold, to the thrust wanted
- | LEVER | Keep out of the 60-92.5% band
use: p40-cutoff
```

> CAUTION: P40 and P42 refuse without a burn set (P30, or P32 to P35) and a loaded time and delta-v. The throttle is yours from ignition, so set the lever to zero before you start.
