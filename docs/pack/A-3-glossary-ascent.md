## A.3 Glossary: ascent

<!--table st-->
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ascent engine | The engine of the upper stage, which lifts the crew off the Moon. It cannot be throttled |
| Insertion | The end of the ascent burn, entering orbit |
| Pitch-over | The end of the vertical rise, when the ship tips downrange |
| Cross-range | How far the landing site is from the CSM's orbit plane |
| Apolune, perilune | The highest and lowest points of a lunar orbit |
| Time of free fall | Time until the path comes down to 35,000 ft above the landing site |
| Coelliptic, short rendezvous | The two ways the crews met the CSM after lift-off: over about 3 hours, or under 2 |
| Residuals, trim | What a burn left to gain, removed with the RCS |
