## 3.15 Rendezvous 210: TPI (P34)

TPI is the burn onto the path that meets the CSM; two midcourses then correct it. The short rendezvous comes straight from insertion, with no CSI or CDH; the coelliptic fallback comes here after CDH. Tracking (optional): {@3-18}.

```checklist
band: TPI TARGETING | P34
- | DSKY | V37E 34E
- | N37 FL | TPI time: V25 N37 E from the PAD, keyed as N33. PRO
n: Direct: TPI about 45 min after insertion, no P33 first. Fallback: the fallback's TPI time
- | N55 FL | R2 elevation: zero (keeps your time). R3 CSM travel 130.00 deg. PRO
v: V06 N55, the angle at your time
- | PRO |
v: V06 N58: perilune after TPI 0.1 nm, TPI burn, braking burn 0.1 ft/s
- | PRO |
v: V06 N81: the TPI burn
- | PRO |
v: V16 N45: R2 time to ignition. R3 -00.01
- | PRO | Final computation: through the displays again
v: V16 N45: R3 middle gimbal angle
- | PRO | Ends P34
goto: RCS burn instead, V37E 41E: {@3-4 3.4 RCS BURN} | here for the midcourse
band: TPI BURN | P42, ascent engine
use: p42-start
n: Ullage 6 s before TIG
use: p42-burn
- NEXT | MIDCOURSE | About 15 min after TPI: {@3-16}
```

> NOTE: This burn aims at a point: the velocity to gain is worked out from where you are, and P42 steers to it. On the RCS, N85 creeps from 30 s before TIG as it is worked out afresh. A keyed N81 moves the aim point. `V06 N52 E` shows your own angle of travel: avoid 170 to 190 deg.
