## 3.14 Rendezvous 099: TPI (P34)

TPI is the burn onto the path that meets the CSM; two midcourses then correct it. TPI on P40 has not been flown.

```checklist
band: TPI TARGETING | P34, about 15 min before TPI  /  CARD: TPI
- | DSKY | V37E 34E
- | N37 FL | TPI time: from P33, or V25 N37 E. PRO
- | N55 FL | R2 elevation: the last one, 26.60 deg at first. R3 CSM travel 130.00 deg. PRO
v: elevation keyed: V06 N37, the time found. Elevation zero: V06 N55, the angle at your time
- | PRO |
if: V05 N09 00611 (NO TPI TIME FOR THE ELEVATION)
  - PRO | Start P34 again
end
v: V06 N58: perilune after TPI 0.1 nm, TPI burn, braking burn 0.1 ft/s
- | PRO |
v: V06 N59: the TPI burn along the line to the CSM
- | PRO |
v: V16 N45: R2 time to ignition. R3 -00.01
- | PRO | Final computation: through the displays again
v: This time N81, then N59
v: V16 N45: R3 middle gimbal angle
- | PRO | Ends P34
band: TPI BURN | on the RCS, or an engine
goto: Engine burn, V37E 42E or 40E: {@3-3 3.3 ENGINE BURNS}
n: On the RCS, as below
use: p41
- NEXT | MIDCOURSE | About 15 min after TPI: {@3-16}
```

> NOTE: At N55 an elevation value finds the TPI time for it; zero keeps your time and shows the angle.

> NOTE: These burns aim at a point. From 30 s before TIG, N85 is worked out afresh from where you are every 2 s, as the real P41 did: it creeps, and shows what is still needed to meet the CSM. A keyed N81 moves the aim point. `V06 N52 E` shows your own angle of travel: avoid 170 to 190 deg.
