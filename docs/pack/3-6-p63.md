## 3.6 P63 Braking

```checklist
use: abort
band: START | P63  /  CARD: PDI
- | MAP VIEW | **Target the CSM** (for aborts)
[J] - | DSKY | V25 N69 E: LANDING SITE CORRECTION, ONLY IF YOU HAVE ONE
[J] n: BEFORE V37E 63E. P63 ADDS IT ONCE, THEN CLEARS IT
- | DSKY | V37E 63E
v: PROG 63, V06 N61 FL
if: OPR ERR
  - | NO PLAN OR SITE LOADED
  - | PLAN AGAIN, SEND
end
- | PRO | SHIP TURNS, THEN WARPS TO TIG-60 S
n: NO PRO: SHIP HOLDS ATTITUDE. P63 GOES ON BY ITSELF AT TIG-35 S
n: KEYING OVER THE DISPLAY LIGHTS KEY REL. PRESS KEY REL TO GIVE IT BACK
band: COUNT-DOWN
- TIG-0:30 | V06 N62 | Counts down
- TIG-0:07.6 | RCS | Ullage
- TIG-0:05 | V99 N62 FL | PRO (ENABLE ENGINE)
n: MISSED IT: V32E SHOWS IT AGAIN
band: BRAKING
- TIG | ENGINE | Lights at low thrust
- TIG+0:26 | ENGINE | Full thrust
[A11] v: V06 N63
[A11] n: NOTHING TO KEY
[J] v: V06 N63 FL
[J] - | DSKY | V57E: PERMIT LANDING RADAR UPDATES. FLASHING STOPS
[J] n: PRO AND V34E DO NOTHING TO THIS DISPLAY. V58E SETS IT FLASHING AGAIN
n: V16 N60 E FOR SPEEDS AND HEIGHT
if: V97 N62 FL (NO THRUST)
  - PRO | RESTART MONITORING
  - ENTR | ENGINE OFF, RELIGHT IN 5 S
  - V34E | END P63
end
n: Times after TIG below are from one flight of each version. Expect differences
[J] - TIG+4:45 | THROTTLE | Down to about 65%. About 41,800 ft (12.7 km)
[A11] - TIG+5:50 | THROTTLE | Down to about 65%. About 30,000 ft (9.1 km)
n: P64 starts by itself at time to go under 62 s
[A11] - TIG+8:20 | P64 | About 7900 ft (2.4 km)
[J] - TIG+9:40 | P64 | About 7900 ft (2.4 km)
- NEXT | APPROACH | {@3-7}
```

> CAUTION: P63 never stages. If you stage the lander, cut the throttle first: one stage turns on the RCS and the descent engine together.

> NOTE: V97 trips on under 0.18 m/s2 for about 20 s after ignition, or about 8 s after the engine had thrust and lost it. `V16 N61 E` shows braking time to go in R2.
