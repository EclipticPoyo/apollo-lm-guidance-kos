## A.4 Glossary: ascent, aborts, rendezvous

<!--table st-->
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Abort, abort stage | Giving up a landing: on the descent engine (P70), or by dropping the descent stage (P71) |
| Ascent engine | The engine of the upper stage, which lifts the crew off the Moon. It cannot be throttled |
| Insertion | The end of the ascent burn, entering orbit |
| Pitch-over | The end of the vertical rise, when the ship tips downrange |
| Cross-range | How far the landing site is from the CSM's orbit plane |
| Apolune, perilune | The highest and lowest points of a lunar orbit |
| Time of free fall | Time until the path comes down to 35,000 ft above the landing site |
| Coelliptic, short rendezvous | The two ways the crews met the CSM after lift-off: over about 3 hours, or under 2 |
| CSI, CDH | Coelliptic sequence initiation and constant delta height: the first two rendezvous burns |
| TPI | Terminal phase initiation, the burn onto the intercept path |
| Aim point | Where a burn must carry you by the intercept time. The velocity to gain follows from it |
| Midcourse | A small correction between TPI and the intercept |
| Mark | One sighting of the CSM taken into the rendezvous navigation; here only counted |
| Elevation angle | How high the CSM stands above your forward horizontal at TPI |
| Line-of-sight rate | How fast the direction to the CSM turns, in thousandths of a radian a second (mrad/s) |
| Cross-pointer | The LM's two-needle instrument; with the rendezvous radar, the line-of-sight rates |
| Braking gates | The closing speeds to be down to at set ranges |
| Residuals, trim | What a burn left to gain, removed with the RCS |
