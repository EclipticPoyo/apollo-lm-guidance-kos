## 3.4 RCS burn and thrust monitor (P41, P47)

For your own burns, and a 210 TPI flown on the RCS ({@3-15}). The burn is already set: by P30 ({@3-2}), or by P32 to P35. The 099 rendezvous and midcourse checklists have the P41 steps written out.

```checklist
band: RCS BURN | P41  /  nose X, right Y, window side Z
use: p41
band: THRUST MONITOR | P47  /  a burn you fly entirely by hand
- | DSKY | V37E 47E
v: V16 N83 FL after about 20 s (velocity gained, ship axes)
- | ENTR | Zeroes the count
- | PRO | Ends P47
```
