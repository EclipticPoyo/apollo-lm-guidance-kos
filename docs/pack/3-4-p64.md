## 3.4 P64 Approach and redesignation

```checklist
band: APPROACH | P64
- | P64 | Starts by itself
v: V06 N64 FL (redesignation open)
n: Aim point shown as a green arrow on the site (flight view) and a pin (map)
- NOW | PRO | Permits redesignation. Flashing stops
- | SAS | Off
- | W / S | Aim point 0.5 deg per click
- | A / D | Aim point 2 deg per click
v: R1 changes with each click
n: A held key is one click
if: KEY MOVES THE WRONG WAY
  - | Flip P64_PITCH_SIGN or P64_YAW_SIGN in agc_config.txt
end
- TTG 34 S | WINDOW | Closes. Terminal: REDESIGNATION CLOSED
- P64+2:40 | P65 | Starts by itself, about 160 s after P64
v: V06 N60
if: ABORT THE PROGRAMME
  - V34E | At flashing N64 ends P64
  - V32E | Shows N64 again
end
```

> NOTE: J-class: each click is 1 deg on both axes. P64 starts shorter and there is no P65: at under 12 s to go P64 hands to P66.
