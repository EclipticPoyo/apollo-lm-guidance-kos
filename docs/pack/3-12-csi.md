## 3.12 Rendezvous 099: CSI (P32)

CSI, at an apolune, and CDH, about half an orbit later, leave your orbit level below the CSM's, closing on it. P32 and P33 work the burns out; P41 flies them. Tracking (optional): {@3-18}.

```fork
Which software?
- 099 | Carry on here: | CSI › CDH › TPI
- 210 | Direct flight: | No CSI or CDH. Skip to page {@3-15}
- 210 | Coelliptic fallback: | Carry on here, then {@3-13} and {@3-15}
```

```checklist
band: BEFORE | in lunar orbit
- | KSP | **Target the CSM**
- | PAD | The CSI and TPI times from the ascent PAD
band: CSI TARGETING | P32
- | DSKY | V37E 32E
- | N11 FL | CSI time: V25 N11 E from the PAD, keyed as N33. PRO
- | N55 FL | R1 1: CDH half an orbit after CSI. R2 26.60 deg: elevation at TPI. PRO
- | N37 FL | TPI time: V25 N37 E from the PAD. PRO
v: V06 N75: CSM height above you 0.1 nm, CSI to CDH, CDH to TPI (MM SS, whole hours dropped)
- | PRO |
v: V06 N81: CSI burn, 0.1 ft/s. R1 forward
- | PRO | Sets this burn for P41 and N33
v: V06 N82: CDH burn as predicted. PRO
v: V16 N45: R2 time to ignition. R3 -00.01
- | PRO | Computes again. PRO through N75, N81, N82
v: V16 N45: R3 middle gimbal angle
- | PRO | Ends P32
```

### P32 alarms

<!--table vt-->
| Alarm | CSI cannot be targeted |
|---|---|
| 600 | No solution on the first try |
| 601, 602 | Perilune after CSI, after CDH, under 35,000 ft (10.7 km) above the site (the Moon's mean radius with no site loaded) |
| 603, 604 | CSI to CDH, CDH to TPI, under 10 minutes. For 604 key a later TPI time |
| 605 | No solution within 16 tries |
| 606 | CSI burn over 1000 ft/s (305 m/s) |

605 or 606 with the PAD's CSI time: V32, then V25 N11 E with all three registers `+00000`. [[PRO]] shows your next apolune, [[PRO]] again accepts it.

At a P32 alarm (`V05 N09`) V32 starts P32 again at N11, `V34E` ends it, [[PRO]] only shows it again. [[RSET]] puts PROG out.

> NOTE: P32 solves CSI so that the CSM is at the elevation angle at your TPI time: a time that does not suit gives a large burn or an alarm. After P34, reload N55 first.

> NOTE: A delta-v keyed at N81 is used by that [[PRO]], but the computing at the first N45 replaces it: key it again.
