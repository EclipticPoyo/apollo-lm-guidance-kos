## 3.5 DOI burn (P30, P40)

The planner's burn that lowers your orbit before PDI. Start with {@3-1} done.

```checklist
band: CHECK THE BURN | P30  /  CARD: DOI
use: p30
band: FLY THE BURN | P40  /  from circular orbit
use: p40-ignition
- TIG+0:15 | TERMINAL | Raise throttle
- | SHIFT | Hold, to about 40% thrust
- | LEVER | Keep out of the 60-92.5% band
use: p40-cutoff
band: THEN | P63
- | MAP VIEW | **Target the CSM**, before P63 (for aborts)
[J] - | DSKY | V25 N69 E: site correction, before P63, only if you have one
- NOW | DSKY | V37E 63E
n: Do not coast to PDI. P63 warps you there
- NEXT | BRAKING | {@3-6}
```

> CAUTION: The throttle is yours from ignition, so set the lever to zero before you start. P40 refuses without a loaded time and delta-v.
