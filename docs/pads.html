LM PADs

The forms and data cards an Apollo LM crew copied figures onto. Type into the boxes, or print the cards and write on them. Printed characters are fixed and typing moves to the next box. What you type is kept in this browser. The cards open short: what nothing here fills is left out, and marks say where figures come from, P copy from the planner, D copy from the DSKY. Full cards shows the real ones. Grey figures are filled in already (some depend on your software version, 099 or 210): type over one if yours differs.

What you type is not saved in this browser: it will be lost when the page is closed. Print the cards to keep it.

The cards are recreated from the Apollo 11 Final Flight Plan, Section II (Update Forms). Entries particular to Apollo 11 are left blank.
